🔌 What is a Physical Device / IoT? — Unlike apps (which only live on your phone), a physical device is something you can actually hold and touch — like a smartwatch, smart speaker, or electric car. These devices have software built inside the hardware itself. "IoT" stands for "Internet of Things" — everyday objects (like a thermostat or fridge) that connect to the internet and can sense, respond, or communicate on their own.
